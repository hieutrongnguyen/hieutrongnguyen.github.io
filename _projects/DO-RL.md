This work applies the approximate/adaptive dynamic programming (ADP) method to solve the optimal control problem for an overhead crane system. Specifically, a disturbance observer is integrated into the controller to compensate for unknown input disturbances.

My role involved implementing the proposed system in MATLAB to evaluate the effectiveness of the control method.

The work is published on the Proceedings of the 2022 11th International Conference on Control, Automation and Information Sciences ([ICCAIS](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9990215)).
